项目概览

01 What problem does your project solve? 你们的项目解决什么问题？

Our project helps people check a product's ownership history and remaining warranty. It also lets them transfer ownership and request warranty service in the same system.

02 Who are the main users of your system? 系统主要给哪些人使用？

The main users are manufacturers, retailers, customers, and service centers. They share the same product record, and each role has different tasks.

03 What does 'product provenance' mean in your project? 你们项目中的「产品溯源」是什么意思？

It means the recorded origin and history of a product. We show who registered it and how ownership moved from the manufacturer to the retailer, the customer, and any later buyers.

04 Can you describe the complete lifecycle of a product in your system? 能介绍产品从登记、销售到保修和转售的完整流程吗？

A manufacturer registers the product and transfers it to a retailer. When the first customer accepts it from the retailer, the warranty starts. The customer can then request service or resell the product.

05 Why do you use blockchain instead of a normal database? 为什么使用区块链，而不是普通数据库？

Manufacturers, retailers, customers, and service centers all need the same product history. We chose blockchain for a shared record, and the smart contract enforces rules such as who can transfer a product.

06 Why did you choose Ethereum instead of Hyperledger Fabric? 为什么选择 Ethereum，而不是 Hyperledger Fabric？

Ethereum let us build and test our smart contract locally with Hardhat. That was simpler for our course demo than setting up a Fabric network with several organizations.

07 Why did you choose Solidity for your smart contract? 为什么使用 Solidity 编写智能合约？

We chose Solidity because our contract runs on Ethereum. It lets us define who can call each function and which changes to ownership and warranty are allowed.

08 How does your system benefit customers? 系统能给消费者带来什么好处？

Customers can check the current owner, ownership history, and warranty status in one place. A second-hand buyer can also see how much warranty remains before accepting the transfer.

09 What advantages does your system have over paper receipts or warranty cards? 相比纸质收据或保修卡，系统有什么优势？

The website shows an up-to-date record of ownership and warranty claims. The record stays with the product after resale, so each buyer can check the same history.

10 Which parts of your system still depend on a trusted organization? 系统中哪些部分仍需要信任机构？

We rely on the administrator to grant roles correctly, manufacturers to enter accurate product details, and service centers to report their work honestly. The contract can check permissions and rules for what they submit.

系统架构

11 Can you explain your system architecture in simple terms? 能简单介绍你们的系统架构吗？

We use a React website and a Solidity smart contract. The website reads the contract through ethers.js, and users sign transactions in MetaMask when they need to update a record.

12 What technologies do you use for the frontend? 前端使用了哪些技术？

We use React and TypeScript for the user interface, Vite for the build, and ethers.js to communicate with the smart contract.

13 What is the role of ethers.js in your system? ethers.js 在系统中起什么作用？

It connects the website to the blockchain. We use it to read contract state and events, and to send write transactions through the user's wallet.

14 What is the role of MetaMask? MetaMask 在系统中起什么作用？

MetaMask lets users select an account and confirm transactions. It signs the transaction with the selected account, while the website uses the public address.

15 Do you have a backend server or a separate database? 你们有业务后端服务器或独立数据库吗？

No. The contract holds the product and claim records. The static website reads directly from the blockchain through an RPC connection.

16 What information is stored on-chain, and what is stored in the browser? 哪些信息保存在链上，哪些信息保存在浏览器？

Product data, ownership, warranty terms, and claim records are on-chain. The browser remembers the contract connection settings, such as its address and deployment block.

17 What is the difference between smart contract state and blockchain events? 合约状态和区块链事件有什么区别？

Contract state tells us what is true now, such as who owns the product. Events log actions that happened, such as a completed transfer. The website uses both to show the full picture.

18 How does the frontend update after a transaction? 交易完成后，前端如何更新数据？

The website waits for a successful transaction receipt and then reads the product data again. If the read fails, it tells the user that the transaction succeeded and asks them to look up the product again.

19 How do you display both the current owner and the ownership history? 如何同时展示当前所有者和所有权历史？

We read the current owner from the contract state. We read completed transfers from blockchain events and display them in order.

20 Can someone verify a product without connecting a wallet? 用户不连接钱包也能查询产品吗？

Yes. Anyone can enter the product ID and serial number to look up its registered record. A wallet is needed for write actions, such as a transfer or a warranty claim.

角色与权限

21 What are the four business roles, and what can each role do? 四种业务角色分别能做什么？

Manufacturers register products. Retailers transfer products to buyers. Customers check records, request warranty service, or resell products. Service centers review claims and record service results.

22 Who becomes the administrator of the contract? 谁会成为合约管理员？

The wallet that deploys the smart contract becomes its administrator. That address is fixed for that deployment.

23 How does the administrator grant or revoke roles? 管理员如何授予或撤销角色？

The administrator enters a team member's wallet address, selects a role, and signs the grant or revoke transaction. The contract checks that the caller is the administrator.

24 Does a customer need to register or receive a role first? 消费者需要先注册或获得角色授权吗？

No. Once the customer accepts the transfer, the contract records their wallet as the current owner. That is how the contract knows who can submit a claim or resell the product.

25 How do you prevent unauthorized users from performing restricted actions? 如何防止用户越权操作？

The smart contract checks the caller's wallet address. For example, only an authorized manufacturer can register a product, and only the current owner can start a transfer.

26 Can someone bypass your security checks by calling the contract directly? 绕过网页直接调用合约，能绕过权限检查吗？

No. The smart contract runs its permission checks for every call. The same checks apply whether the caller uses our website or another tool.

27 Can one wallet have more than one organization role? 同一个钱包能否拥有多个机构角色？

Yes. Manufacturer, retailer, and service center permissions are stored separately, so the administrator can grant several roles to one wallet. For the demo, we use different accounts to make the roles clear.

28 How do you prevent duplicate product IDs or serial numbers? 如何防止重复的产品编号或序列号？

The contract checks the product ID and serial number separately. It rejects the registration if either one has already been used.

29 Can the manufacturer change product details or warranty terms after registration? 登记后，制造商还能修改产品资料或保修条款吗？

No. Fields such as the model, serial number, warranty duration, and claim limit are fixed after registration. Ownership and claim history still change through their own operations.

30 What happens if the manufacturer registers incorrect information? 如果制造商登记了错误信息，怎么办？

The current contract has no edit or delete feature for a registered product. The manufacturer needs to check the details before signing. A production version could add a separate correction process with an audit trail.

所有权转让

31 Why do you use a two-step ownership transfer? 为什么所有权转让要分两步？

The current owner starts the transfer, and the selected recipient accepts it. This gives the recipient a chance to check the transfer before ownership changes.

32 Who is allowed to start an ownership transfer? 谁可以发起所有权转让？

Only the wallet recorded as the current owner can start a transfer. It must give a valid recipient address that follows the rules for the product's current stage.

33 Who can accept or reject a pending transfer? 谁可以接收或拒绝待处理的转让？

Only the selected recipient can accept or reject it. If the sender wants to stop it, the current owner can use Cancel.

34 What happens if the recipient never accepts the transfer? 如果接收方一直不确认，会怎样？

The owner can cancel it, and the recipient can reject it. The default deadline is ten minutes. Once it expires, anyone can clear it, and the owner can start again.

35 Who owns the product while the transfer is still pending? 转让尚未确认时，产品属于谁？

The original owner still owns the product. The contract updates ownership only after the selected recipient accepts the transfer.

36 Can a user transfer a product to themselves or to a zero address? 能把产品转给自己或零地址吗？

No. The contract rejects the zero address and the current owner's own address as recipients.

37 Can a manufacturer transfer a product directly to a customer? 制造商能否直接把产品转给消费者？

No. The first transfer must go to an authorized retailer. The retailer then transfers the product to a customer.

38 What happens if the recipient's role changes before acceptance? 如果接收方角色在确认前发生变化，会怎样？

The contract checks the recipient's role again when they accept. If the role no longer fits the transfer rules, acceptance fails and the owner stays the same.

39 Does changing the transfer timeout affect transfers that already exist? 修改转让超时会影响已经发起的转让吗？

No. Each transfer gets its own deadline when it starts. The updated timeout applies only to new transfers.

40 How can you prove that an ownership transfer has completed? 如何证明一笔所有权转让已经完成？

We can check for a successful receipt from the acceptance transaction. We can also confirm that the contract shows the new owner and that the transfer appears in the ownership history.

保修规则

41 When does the warranty start? 保修什么时候开始？

It starts when the first customer accepts the transfer from the retailer. The contract saves that time and calculates the warranty end date.

42 Who sets the warranty duration and claim limit? 谁设置保修期限和理赔次数上限？

The authorized manufacturer sets both when registering the product. The terms stay fixed for that registered product.

43 How does the system determine whether a warranty is still valid? 系统如何判断保修是否仍然有效？

The warranty must have started, the product must be within the warranty period, and there must be a claim left. The contract checks these conditions on submission and approval.

44 Does warranty expiration require a scheduled task or an extra transaction? 保修到期需要定时任务或额外交易吗？

No. The contract uses the block timestamp to check the deadline when someone submits or approves a claim. The page also updates the displayed status as time passes.

45 When is the claim count increased: submission, approval, or servicing? 理赔次数是在提交、批准还是服务完成时增加？

The count increases when the service center approves the claim. Submission and recording the service result do not use another claim.

46 Does a rejected claim use up the warranty allowance? 被拒绝的申请会消耗保修次数吗？

No. Only approved claims use the allowance. After rejection, the current owner can submit a new claim if warranty coverage is available.

47 Can a customer submit a claim before the warranty starts? 保修未激活时能提交理赔吗？

No. The contract requires an active warranty before it accepts a claim. In our system, activation happens when the first customer accepts the product from the retailer.

48 How do you convert warranty months into time in the contract? 前端输入的保修月数如何换算成合约时间？

The form counts one month as 30 days and sends the duration in seconds. For example, 24 months in the form means 720 days.

49 Does reselling a product restart its warranty? 产品转卖后，保修会重新计算吗？

No. The warranty belongs to the product. Resale keeps the original start date, end date, and approved-claim count.

50 Does the new owner receive the remaining warranty and claim allowance? 新所有者能继承剩余的保修期限和次数吗？

Yes. The buyer takes over whatever time and claim allowance the product has left. They can submit a claim if the warranty still has coverage.

理赔与维修

51 Can you explain the complete warranty claim process? 能介绍完整的保修申请流程吗？

The current owner selects a problem type and submits a claim. An authorized service center approves or rejects it. After providing the service, a service center records the result.

52 Can someone who is not the current owner submit a claim? 非当前所有者能否提交理赔？

No. The contract compares the caller's wallet address with the current owner. If the addresses do not match, it rejects the submission.

53 What types of problems can a customer report? 消费者能选择哪些问题类型？

The options are Screen, Battery, Software, and Other. We use fixed categories so the claim can be recorded without a detailed description.

54 What is the difference between Pending, Approved, and Serviced? 待处理、已批准和已完成服务有什么区别？

Pending means the claim is waiting for review. Approved means the service center accepted it. Serviced means a service center recorded the outcome of the service.

55 Can one product have several pending claims at the same time? 同一产品能有多个待处理申请吗？

No. Each product can have only one pending claim at a time. Once that claim is approved, rejected, or cancelled by a transfer, it is no longer pending.

56 Can any authorized service center process a claim? 任意已授权服务中心都可以处理申请吗？

Yes. Any currently authorized service center can review a pending claim. The contract still checks the claim status and, for approval, the remaining warranty coverage.

57 What information do you store for each claim? 每笔理赔保存哪些信息？

The claim is tied to a product and has an ID, the applicant's wallet, a problem type, a submission time, and a status. It also records who processed or serviced it, when they did so, and the service result, when available.

58 What service results can a service center record? 服务中心可以登记哪些结果？

The result can be Repaired, Replaced, Refunded, or Other. The service center selects one when it completes an approved claim.

59 Can the smart contract guarantee that an approved repair actually happens? 合约能保证获批的维修一定会发生吗？

No. Approval means the claim was accepted. The contract can record the result reported by the service center, but it cannot check the physical repair itself.

60 What happens if someone tries to approve or complete the same claim twice? 重复批准或重复完成同一申请会怎样？

The contract rejects the second action. Approval requires a pending claim, and recording the service result requires an approved claim. The status changes after each successful action.

边界场景

61 What happens to a pending claim when ownership is transferred? 所有权转移后，旧的待处理申请怎么办？

The contract cancels the pending claim when the new owner accepts the transfer. The old claim remains visible in the history. The new owner can submit a claim if coverage is available.

62 What happens to an approved claim when the product is resold? 转卖后，已经批准的申请怎么办？

The approved claim stays with the product and can still be completed. The claim allowance it already used stays used after resale.

63 Can the new owner see the previous owner's claim and service history? 新所有者能看到之前的理赔和维修历史吗？

Yes. Claims and service results are linked to the product, so the history remains available after ownership changes.

64 Can a service center process a claim while an ownership transfer is pending? 转让还未确认时，服务中心能处理申请吗？

Yes. The sender is still the current owner until the recipient accepts. An authorized service center can approve or reject the pending claim if the required conditions are met.

65 What happens if claim approval and transfer acceptance occur close together? 批准理赔和确认转让几乎同时发生，会怎样？

The result depends on the order the blockchain executes them. If approval comes first, the approved claim stays with the product. If acceptance comes first, the pending claim is cancelled and can no longer be approved.

66 Can a pending claim be approved after the warranty expires? 保修到期后，还能批准之前提交的待处理申请吗？

No. The contract checks the warranty again at approval time. An authorized service center can still reject the pending claim.

67 Can an approved claim be completed after the warranty expires? 保修到期后，已批准的申请还能完成服务吗？

Yes. An approved claim can still be completed. The warranty deadline applies to new claims and approvals.

68 Can approved servicing continue after the claim limit is reached? 额度用尽后，已批准的维修还能继续吗？

Yes. The claim already used its allowance when it was approved. Recording its service result does not use another claim.

69 Can a service center finish an earlier approved claim after its role is revoked? 服务中心被撤销角色后，还能完成自己此前批准的理赔吗？

Yes. It can complete a claim it already approved. Once revoked, it cannot approve or reject new claims.

70 Must the service center that completes the repair be the one that approved it? 维修完成必须由最初批准理赔的服务中心登记吗？

No. Any currently authorized service center can complete an approved claim. The original approving center can also complete it, even if its role was later revoked.

隐私与信任

71 How do you balance transparency and privacy? 如何平衡透明性与隐私？

We put product identifiers, wallet addresses, and warranty history on-chain so people can verify the records. We leave out names, contact details, photos, and detailed repair reports.

72 Do you store names, phone numbers, photos, or detailed repair reports on-chain? 是否在链上保存姓名、电话、照片或详细维修报告？

No. We store the information needed to track ownership and claims, such as wallet addresses, problem categories, and service results. We use fictional products and test wallets for the demo.

73 Are wallet addresses completely anonymous? 钱包地址完全匿名吗？

No. The address appears in the public record and can be linked to a person if someone knows who controls that wallet.

74 Can your system prove that a physical product is genuine? 系统能证明实物一定是真品吗？

No. We can check the registered record and its ownership history. If a fake item copies a real serial number, the lookup alone cannot tell the two physical items apart.

75 What if an authorized manufacturer enters false information? 如果授权制造商录入虚假信息，怎么办？

The contract can verify who submitted the information and whether the fields pass its rules. The accuracy of the real-world details still depends on the manufacturer and any physical checks.

76 Is your system fully decentralized if it has one administrator? 只有一个管理员，系统还算完全去中心化吗？

The administrator manages organization roles, so that part is centralized. Product records and transactions stay on-chain, where the contract applies the same rules to every caller.

77 What happens if the administrator loses access to their wallet? 管理员丢失钱包访问权限会怎样？

The existing roles and product records remain. The contract has no way to replace the administrator, so no one can grant or revoke roles on that deployment without access to the administrator wallet.

78 Can you upgrade or modify the deployed smart contract? 已部署的合约能升级或修改吗？

Our contract has no upgrade mechanism. If we change its code, we need a new deployment and a plan to move any records we want to keep.

79 How can users check that a successful action was really recorded on-chain? 如何确认页面显示成功的操作确实上链了？

They can check the transaction hash and its receipt on the correct network. They can also read the contract again to confirm the updated owner or claim status.

80 Does recording 'Refunded' automatically send money to the customer? 登记「已退款」会自动转钱给消费者吗？

No. Refunded is a service result stored on-chain. The actual payment is handled outside our system.

钱包与部署

81 How do you handle a wallet connected to the wrong account or network? 钱包连错账户或网络时怎么处理？

The site checks the target network before a write. If the account is wrong for the action, the contract rejects it and the site helps the user switch to the correct wallet account.

82 What feedback does the user receive after clicking an invalid action? 点错按钮后会收到什么提示？

The page shows a message next to the action explaining what went wrong. For example, if the wrong account tries to accept a transfer, the message explains that only the selected recipient can accept it.

83 What happens if the user rejects a wallet request or clicks a button repeatedly? 拒绝钱包请求或重复点击会怎样？

If the user rejects the wallet request, the page shows that the action was cancelled. While a write is in progress, the relevant controls are disabled to avoid sending it twice.

84 What if the transaction succeeds but the frontend fails to refresh? 交易成功但网页刷新失败怎么办？

We tell the user that the transaction was confirmed and the page could not refresh. They can look up the product again without resending the transaction.

85 What is the difference between your Hardhat demo and Sepolia testing? Hardhat 本地演示与 Sepolia 测试有什么区别？

Hardhat runs a local test chain for our demo. Sepolia is a public test network where teammates can connect to the same deployed contract from different computers.

86 Is deploying the website the same as deploying the smart contract? 部署网站和部署智能合约是一回事吗？

They are separate steps. GitHub Pages hosts the interface. The contract must be deployed to the selected chain, and the website connects to that contract address.

87 What happens to the data when you restart the local Hardhat node? 重启本地 Hardhat 节点后数据会怎样？

In our demo setup the local chain starts fresh. We run the deployment script again to set up the contract, demo roles, and sample product.

88 Why do transactions use gas, and does your demo require real ETH? 为什么交易需要 gas？演示需要真实 ETH 吗？

Gas measures the work needed to execute a transaction. Our local demo uses simulated ETH, and Sepolia uses test ETH. A read-only product lookup does not require a paid transaction.

89 Do you need to redeploy the contract every time someone uses the website? 每次有人使用网站都需要重新部署合约吗？

No. Once the contract is deployed, everyone on the same network can reuse its address. We redeploy when we start a fresh local chain or need a new version of the contract.

90 What happens if the RPC service or network is unavailable during the demonstration? 演示时 RPC 或网络不可用怎么办？

We first check that the page and wallet are set to the right network. If the public RPC is unavailable, we can use the local Hardhat demo. If the local node has stopped, we restart it and initialize the demo again.

测试与分工

91 How did you test the system? 你们如何测试系统？

We ran automated tests for permissions, transfers, and warranty rules. We also used MetaMask in a local browser run to test transfers, claim approval, and recording a repair result.

92 Which invalid actions and boundary cases did you test? 你们测了哪些非法操作和边界情况？

We tested cases such as duplicate registration, an unauthorized transfer, an expired transfer, and an expired warranty. We also checked what happens when a pending claim is still open at the time of resale.

93 What is the difference between automated frontend tests and real wallet testing? 前端自动化测试和真实钱包测试有什么区别？

Frontend tests can simulate contract responses to check how the page behaves. In a real wallet test we switch accounts, confirm requests in MetaMask, and check the local chain receipts.

94 How did you measure gas usage, and what did you do to reduce costs? 怎么测 gas？怎样减少成本？

We read the gas used from transaction receipts. The first customer's acceptance used about 58,000 gas in one local run. We keep the current owner in state and use events for past transfers to avoid a separate list of past owners.

95 What might become slow if the system has many products or a long history? 如果产品很多或历史很长，哪些部分可能变慢？

Loading a long ownership or claim history can take longer because the page reads the records from the chain. We could add pagination or an index for larger deployments.

96 What was the most difficult problem, and how did you solve it? 最困难的问题是什么？如何解决？

One problem was that a recipient's role could change after a transfer started. We added a second role check when they accept and tested that a failed acceptance leaves ownership and warranty records unchanged.

97 What was your individual contribution to the project? 你个人负责了什么？

I mainly worked on [your actual part]. I implemented [one specific feature] and checked it by [one test or demo step you actually did].

98 How did the five team members collaborate and check each other's work? 五位组员如何协作、检查彼此的工作？

We organized the work around product and ownership logic, warranty logic, the product interface, the warranty interface, and integration. We used shared acceptance criteria to check how the parts fit together.

99 What are the main limitations of the current version? 当前版本有哪些主要局限？

One administrator manages organization roles. We also rely on manufacturers and service centers for real-world facts, such as whether the physical item matches its record and whether a repair actually happened.

100 What would you improve if you had more time? 有更多时间会改进什么？

I would improve the link between the physical product and its digital record, for example by using a tamper-resistant tag. I would also add pagination for longer histories.
